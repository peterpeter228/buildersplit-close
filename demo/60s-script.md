# BuilderSplit Close: 60-second demo

BuilderSplit Close creates a local weekly fee and payout close report, plus a CSV of differences to review.

This demo uses a synthetic sample, not a real ledger. It is a synthetic diagnostic only, not evidence that the tool is ready for real ledgers.

First, open the sample inputs and rules to see which fees and payout shares the example expects.

Second, run the tool locally on your machine.

Third, inspect the report and the CSV. Compare the expected and recorded amounts, and look at the differences before drawing conclusions.

Would you give us permission to use a read-only sample for a local diagnostic? The data stays on your machine. We do not need your keys, and the tool does not move money.

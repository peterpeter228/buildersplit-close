# Exact real-input gap and safe handoff

This is a preparation checklist, not an external request already sent. No customer has authorized or supplied these inputs. Do not commit actual ledgers, customer documents, credentials, or customer identity mapping to this repository.

## Minimum bundle needed before a real adapter can be validated

1. Written permission from a data owner for this specific local reconciliation purpose and retention period. Confirm whether any addresses or transaction records may leave their own machine; otherwise run locally under their control.
2. One Builder, one already completed UTC seven-day interval `[start,end)`, documented network and approved market/fee-asset metadata. At most 50K fills/100 beneficiaries. Begin with one representative small golden subset but disclose its non-complete diagnostic scope.
3. Original export schema and source/version; stable fill identity and uniqueness scope, millisecond timestamps, explicit Builder attribution evidence, fee asset and verified gross vs kept definition. Public 14-column Builder CSV alone is insufficient. Do not join unidentified fills by time/price/size.
4. Source completeness evidence for the agreed scope: query range, terminal pagination/watermark, relevant control totals and their derivation. An assertion of completeness or file hash alone is not proof.
5. Effective-dated attribution and frozen rule version: fee basis, beneficiary pseudonyms, rate, rounding stage/scale/mode and confirmation. Keep any identity mapping on the owner's side.
6. Explicit signed opening balances and independent period-end statement at identical UTC cutoffs, including explicit zero values, source/version and owner confirmation. A copy of our calculated output cannot serve as an independent statement.
7. Confirmed external payment evidence with unique transfer-event identity, amount/asset, beneficiary, occurrence time and obligation/group mapping. Include allocation snapshot with other active groups' usage, even if explicitly zero. Never send wallet secrets or signing capability.
8. Native split activity remains unsupported until matched real credits, fee semantics, rounding and residual calibration exist. Prefer a genuinely no-native scope only if the source can substantiate that fact; do not delete native rows to manufacture eligibility.
9. Owner/independent reviewer hand-calculated golden results for the subset, with expected differences and known omissions. Commercial interviews, payments and repeat use remain separate evidence.

## Safe collection

First obtain explicit permission and agree the minimum files, destination and purpose. Use an existing private approved folder or local-only workspace, never a public repository or external model prompt. Pseudonymize recipient/account identifiers consistently while preserving relationships; remove personal contact information and irrelevant transactions. Preserve hashes and source evidence in the authorized private workspace. Keep raw originals and mapping separate from fixtures. Synthetic fixtures may be committed; real records may not be relabeled synthetic.

No new credentials, customer-system integrations, external outreach, uploads, actual payments or signatures are authorized by this checklist. If permission or completeness is unavailable, keep G0 partial and stop production-adapter/feature expansion. A fully synthetic pass can establish software behavior, not a real ledger or buyer demand.

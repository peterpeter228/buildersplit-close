# 本地对账许可函模板 / Local reconciliation permission letter template

**仅供填写和审阅；未收到许可或记录，不表示已签署、事先同意或授权生效。**
**Template for completion and review only: no permission received, no records supplied, no signature, prior agreement or authorization in force implied.**

所有待填项均为 `[ ]`；这里只填组织或角色标签，不填真实姓名、地址、邮箱或密钥。
Every fill-in is `[ ]`; use organisation or role labels here, not real names, addresses, emails or keys.

## 拟议许可条款 / Proposed permission terms

- **用途仅限本地对账**：比较记录的费用、应付金额和已付金额；不改变账目、不操作资金。限一个 Builder、一个已结束的 UTC 七天周期，最多 50,000 笔成交、100 位收款方；小样本不能当作完整账目。
  **Local reconciliation only:** compare recorded fees, amounts owed and amounts already paid; do not alter books or move money. One Builder, one completed UTC seven-day period, at most 50,000 trades and 100 payees; a subset must not be presented as complete books.
- 数据留在所有者机器上并由其控制，仅限上列本地角色和位置。用一致标签替换身份标识并保持记录关系；身份对照表由所有者单独保管。不收集个人联系方式、密钥、签名权限、无关交易或广泛账本；不向本仓库、公开位置或外部模型上传真实记录。
  Keep data on the owner's machine under their control, limited to the listed local roles and locations. Replace identifiers with consistent labels preserving relationships; the owner keeps the identity key separately. Do not collect personal contact data, keys, signing access, unrelated transactions or broad ledgers; do not upload real records to this repository, public locations or external models.
- **可随时撤回许可**：所有者通过上列约定联系人角色及渠道发出撤回通知；收到后立即停止处理，按上列本地删除步骤及路径移除保留的输入、衍生报告及全部副本。到达保留期限也执行同样删除。渠道和删除步骤须在任何使用前填明。
  **Revocable at any time:** the owner sends a revocation notice through the agreed contact role and channel above. On receipt, stop processing immediately and remove retained inputs, derived reports and all copies using the listed local deletion procedure and path. Perform the same deletion when retention ends. Specify the channel and deletion procedure before any use.

填写模板本身不构成许可；任何处理前仍须由数据所有者明确授权。未授权或资料不完整时，停止真实数据验证，不扩大真实数据接入。
Completing this template is not permission; the data owner must explicitly authorize before any processing. Without permission or complete inputs, stop real-data validation and do not expand real-data connections.
